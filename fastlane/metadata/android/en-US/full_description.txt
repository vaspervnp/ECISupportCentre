One million signatures from at least seven EU countries can ask the European Commission to propose a new law. ECI Support Centre helps you follow every European Citizens' Initiative, sign the ones you support and find out how they end.

• All initiatives in one place
Browse every registered initiative: collecting signatures, starting soon, successful, answered by the Commission, not enough support or withdrawn. Search, filter and sort them, and follow the ones you care about.

• Sign on the official website
"Sign this initiative" takes you straight to the European Commission's official collection page. The app never collects signatures or personal data.

• Never miss a deadline
Get a notification a week and a day before a collection ends, when an initiative reaches 100,000, 250,000, 500,000 or one million supporters, and when it succeeds, fails or gets the Commission's answer. You choose: every initiative, or only the ones you follow.

• News
The latest news from the official European Citizens' Initiative website.

• Works offline
Everything is kept on your device and updated in the background, as often as you like, or only on Wi-Fi.

• In your language
The app is available in all 24 official EU languages, and shows each initiative in your language when a translation exists.

• Private by design
No account, no ads, no tracking. What you follow or mark as signed stays on your device.

Also: light and dark theme, TalkBack support, large text, and a layout for tablets.
