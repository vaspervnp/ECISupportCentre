# Privacy policy: ECI Support Centre

*Last updated: 16 November 2026*

ECI Support Centre is a free, open-source Android app, created by the Pirate Party of Greece, for following the European Citizens' Initiatives. It is not an official app of the European Union or the European Commission.

**In short:** the app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It collects no personal data, and nothing you do in the app is sent to us.

## What stays on your device

The app keeps a local database and settings on your phone or tablet:

- the initiatives, their details and the news, downloaded from the European Commission's websites;
- which initiatives you follow and which ones you marked as signed;
- your settings (language, theme, how often to update, Wi-Fi only, notifications).

This data never leaves your device through the app. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. If you turned on Android's own backup, Android may include it in your device backup with your Google account; that backup is handled by Google, not by us.

## What the app connects to

The app downloads public data only from the European Commission's websites:

- `register.eci.ec.europa.eu`: the public register of initiatives (list, details, logos);
- `citizens-initiative.europa.eu`: the news feed and its images.

Like any website you visit, these servers see your IP address and the app's name and version (`EciSupportCentre/1.0.0`). The European Commission's [privacy policy for the European Citizens' Initiative website](https://citizens-initiative.europa.eu/how-it-works/privacy-policy_en) applies to them.

## Signing an initiative

The app never collects signatures. "Sign this initiative" opens the Commission's official online collection system (`eci.ec.europa.eu`) in your browser. Whatever you enter there goes to the Commission and the organisers under their privacy statements; the app does not see it. Marking an initiative as signed in the app only stores a checkmark on your device.

## Notifications

Notifications (new initiatives, collections ending, results, news) are created on your device after each update. There is no push server and no notification ID is sent anywhere. You can turn them off in the app's settings or in Android's settings.

## Sharing

When you share an initiative, the app hands its title and the link to its official page to the app you choose. Nothing else is shared.

## Permissions

- **Internet and network state**: to download the public data and to know whether you are online.
- **Notifications** (Android 13 and later): to show the notifications above. You can refuse it; the app works without it.

## Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published in this file, with a new date at the top. The source code is public at [github.com/vaspervnp/ECISupportCentre](https://github.com/vaspervnp/ECISupportCentre). For questions, open an issue there.
